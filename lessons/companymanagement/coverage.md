# POI 1.3 Company Management

Primary course source in the Leadership Phase folder: `3. Company Management.pdf` (60 pages). Citations use its original 1-based page order.

Pages 16–17, 19, 21–24, 27–32, 35–40 and 44–50 cover company command foundations, responsibilities, functional roles and command practices. Page 7 states the classroom outcome and page 14 identifies the Company Commander's Guide Handbook (2020) as the lesson reference.

The course's named organizations, supplied group scenarios and essay assessment are outside the banks. Questions on discipline stay within the lesson's general principles.

Each difficulty has 20 questions. Easy checks recall and recognition. Medium applies a principle in a classroom situation. Hard asks students to diagnose competing explanations, weigh tradeoffs, or integrate principles. The scenarios were written for this practice.
