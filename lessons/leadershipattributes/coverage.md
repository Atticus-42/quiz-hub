# POI 1.1 Military Leadership Attributes

Primary course source in the Leadership Phase folder: `1. Military Leadership Attributes.pdf` (49 pages). Citations use its original 1-based page order.

Pages 14–22, 25–29, 31 and 33–36 cover the three leader attributes and their components. The practice uses the course's Philippine Army Core Values and fictional platoon-level situations.

Film viewing, classroom administration and the written assessment are outside the question banks.

Each difficulty has 20 questions. Easy checks recall and recognition. Medium applies a principle in a classroom situation. Hard asks students to diagnose competing explanations, weigh tradeoffs, or integrate principles. The scenarios were written for this practice.
