# POI 8.5 C4ISTAR Communications Planning

Primary course source: `2. IMM C4ISTAR Communication Planning.pptx` (50 slides). Citations use its original 1-based slide order.

Slides 14–31, 33–34, 36–39 and 42–44 support the bank. Coverage includes requirements, technology purposes, integration of conceptual component roles, and standards, reliability, security, and scalability. Architecture is tested at the level of component roles because the deck summarizes architecture on slide 45 without giving a detailed design method.

The bank omits actual orders, call signs, frequencies, cryptographic material, personal details, network configurations, operational particulars, and weapon or electronic-warfare employment details.

Each difficulty has 20 questions. Easy checks recall and recognition. Medium applies a principle in a classroom situation. Hard asks students to diagnose competing explanations, weigh tradeoffs, or integrate principles. The scenarios were written for this practice.
