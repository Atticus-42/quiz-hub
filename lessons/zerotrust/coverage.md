# Zero Trust: coverage and source review

POI 7.2. Primary source: **2. Zero Trust.pptx** (31 slides). References use the file's actual page/slide position, not printed slide footers. Source files are not published.

## Scope and qualifications

Substantive source coverage is slides 19–27, with purpose at slide 8. Actual deck positions are used, not footer numbering. Biographies, icebreakers, safety reminders and blank/closing slides are excluded. NIST SP 800-207 is the primary reference named by the deck; roles were checked against NIST's official final publication and implementation architecture. The slide's strong claims that ZTA prevents lateral movement or that MFA stops stolen-credential risk are taught as risk reduction, not guarantees. Encrypted-traffic metadata can inform detection but does not guarantee visibility into every malicious action. No claim is made that a product label proves conformance. This quiz supplements, rather than replaces, the assigned essay assessment. External clarification: [NIST SP 800-207](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-207.pdf) and [NIST implementation architecture](https://pages.nist.gov/zero-trust-architecture/VolumeB/architecture.html).

## Assessment design

Easy checks comprehension, Medium applies concepts to a stated case, and Hard compares explanations or decisions under constraints. Each mode samples every declared topic. Four-option questions use one best answer; answer positions are balanced. Review full sources as well as the quiz.
