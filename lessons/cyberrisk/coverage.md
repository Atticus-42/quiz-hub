# Cyber Risk Assessment: coverage and source review

POI 7.4. Primary source: **4. Cyber Risk Assessment.pdf** (47 pages). References use the file's actual page/slide position, not printed slide footers. Source files are not published.

## Scope and qualifications

The six phases on pages 18–24 describe incident response even though the slide heading says Risk Management Process; they are not presented here as the whole risk-management discipline. The SANS 20 controls on pages 25–28 are the lesson's legacy list, not a claim about the latest CIS Controls. Page 30's percentages, weights and formula are classroom-specific, not universal risk thresholds. Zero and fractional boundary gaps are unspecified; do not invent a classification. Arithmetic questions explicitly provide the inputs and weights, avoiding inference from incomplete assessment worksheets. Local organizational assignments and timing examples are taught as lesson content, not current command orders. Authorized defensive assessment only; no exploitation instructions. All 20 legacy control areas appear across the questions/explanations; the full lesson remains required reading.

## Assessment design

Easy checks comprehension, Medium applies concepts to a stated case, and Hard compares explanations or decisions under constraints. Each mode samples every declared topic. Four-option questions use one best answer; answer positions are balanced. Review full sources as well as the quiz.
