# POI 8.1 CEOI / CESI Management

Primary course source: `CEOI CESI.pdf` (53 pages). Citations use its original 1-based page order.

Pages 9–18, 21–22, 25–26, 28–31, 33, 39–41, 45 and 47 support the bank. Coverage includes the roles of the two publications, command and staff responsibility, edition status, copy accountability, compromise concepts, and document validation.

The bank omits actual orders, call signs, frequencies, cryptographic material, personal details, network configurations, operational particulars, and the graded practical exercise.

Each difficulty has 20 questions. Easy checks recall and recognition. Medium applies a principle in a classroom situation. Hard asks students to diagnose competing explanations, weigh tradeoffs, or integrate principles. The scenarios were written for this practice.
