Item ids: E = easy.json, M = medium.json, H = hard.json. (S) = scenario-to-term item, (O) = objective item.
Totals: easy 46 (15 S / 31 O), medium 47 (19 S / 28 O), hard 44 (27 S / 17 O), giving 137 items (61 S / 76 O). H44 was added at merge so that every bank covers every topic (hard had no Operational Environment item).
