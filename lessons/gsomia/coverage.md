# LOE3 GSOMIA Compliance: coverage and source review

POI 7.3. Primary source: **3. LOE3 GSOMIA Compliance.pptx** (52 slides). References use the file's actual page/slide position, not printed slide footers. Source files are not published.

## Scope and qualifications

The supplied lesson teaches general information-protection principles, not the full text of a particular agreement. Questions do not authorize release, establish legal obligations, or prescribe classification levels for real information. Use the applicable agreement and competent authority for actual decisions. Unclassified information is not described as a classified level. Instructor profiles and administrative slides are excluded. The 20 questions in each mode cover governance, classification/storage/transmission/disposal, personnel and role-based access, sharing records, incidents, audits, training, physical/cyber protection and improvement. This practice does not replace the lesson's written evaluation.

## Assessment design

Easy checks comprehension, Medium applies concepts to a stated case, and Hard compares explanations or decisions under constraints. Each mode samples every declared topic. Four-option questions use one best answer; answer positions are balanced. Review full sources as well as the quiz.
