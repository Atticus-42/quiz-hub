# C4ISTAR Sensors Integration: coverage and source review

POI 5.4. Primary source: **4. C4ISTAR Sensors Integration.pdf** (35 pages). References use the file's actual page/slide position, not printed slide footers. Source files are not published.

## Scope and qualifications

Paired source: 4. Handout C4ISTAR Sensors Integration.pdf, seven pages. Quiz references point to actual pages of the 35-page presentation. Questions cover the five ELOs, using classroom and disaster-response scenarios rather than targeting procedures. Equipment-role descriptions are lesson content, not a statement about current inventory. Page 32's contradictory 'five (3)' requirement and conflicting publication dates are not assessed. RDF bearings do not guarantee an exact location; uncertainty and geometry matter. Integration and interoperability are related but distinct; testing supplies evidence, not the definition of interoperability. Technical controls such as redundancy and shielding are not guarantees of immunity. Biographies, real deployment details and weapon employment procedures are excluded. Practical/group-presentation outcomes require instructor assessment beyond these questions.

## Assessment design

Easy checks comprehension, Medium applies concepts to a stated case, and Hard compares explanations or decisions under constraints. Each mode samples every declared topic. Four-option questions use one best answer; answer positions are balanced. Review full sources as well as the quiz.
