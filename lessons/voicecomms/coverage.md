# POI 8.3 coverage

**Primary reference:** SOAC 52-2026 *Voice Communications Planning* student handout (ELOs 8.3.1 to 8.3.4), converted from the supplied Word file to a 10-page PDF (`library/files/lesson-voice-communications-planning.pdf`). Numbered references in the bank are PDF pages of that file. The handout quotes PAM 6-0102 *Signal Operations*, PAM 6-00 *C4S* and PAM 3-00 with paragraph numbers; the bank cites the handout pages, and the three manuals are linked in the Library as cited references. Each difficulty has 28 questions with permanent qids `voicecomms-e/m/h-01` to `-28`. Easy and Medium are mostly objective (sources, fields, means, bands, steps, facilities, specialties). Hard applies the same rules to the handout's fictional Task Force TALA case (situation, assets, injects and evaluation, pages 6 to 9).

**Answer balance:** each bank has 7 keys at every letter. The correct answer is the unique longest option in 6 (easy), 7 (medium) and 8 (hard) of 28 items, under the 35% limit. Prompts are unique across the three banks, and distractors are drawn from other rows of the same handout tables where possible.

**Limits.**
- The handout marks some content *Supplementary* (requirement fields, choosing a technology, PACE failure modes, net structure, Annexes A to C). It is tested as the handout's teaching guidance, and explanations do not present it as doctrine.
- The case scenario has no model answer. Hard items test only what the handout states (band uses, line of sight, the 0406 table, recovery rules, the disaster-net rule, maintenance tasks, specialties, the task list and the scoring rule). They do not ask for a group's plan or for figures the scenario leaves to assumption.
- The six review questions on page 6 are covered by the bank's own items. The assets table quantities on page 7 are used only as case context and are not asked as recall.
- METAL is the only estimate content in this handout; the full Annex A estimate format belongs to POI 8.2 and the Data Communications Planning handout.
