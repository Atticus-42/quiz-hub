# MANET C4ISTAR Planning: coverage and source review

POI 5.3. Primary source: **3. MANET C4ISTAR Planning.pdf** (82 pages). References use the file's actual page/slide position, not printed slide footers. Source files are not published.

## Scope and qualifications

This bank assesses conceptual network planning, supported capabilities, equipment roles, safe maintenance and authorized diagnostic reasoning. It does not reproduce real network addresses, deployment layouts, operational parameters, key-loading/erase sequences or targeting procedures. Page 33 repeats the WB heading despite different specifications; that inconsistent label and product capacity trivia are not assessed. E-LynX capabilities are described as source-specific, not properties of every MANET. Self-healing and frequency hopping are not guarantees against disconnection or interference. Password/menu details are conceptual only; students must use approved equipment instructions for practical operation. The 20 questions per mode cover all substantive conceptual sections; practical equipment competence requires supervised assessment.

## Assessment design

Easy checks comprehension, Medium applies concepts to a stated case, and Hard compares explanations or decisions under constraints. Each mode samples every declared topic. Four-option questions use one best answer; answer positions are balanced. Review full sources as well as the quiz.
