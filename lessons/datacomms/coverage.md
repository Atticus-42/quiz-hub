**Answer balance:** each bank has 7 keys at every letter. The correct answer is the unique longest option in 7 (easy), 9 (medium) and 9 (hard) of 28 items, within the 35% limit. Prompts are unique across the three banks.

**Limits.**
- The handout marks some content *Supplementary* (quantifying requirements, the means comparison, network vocabulary, the data appendix, voice sizing figures). It is tested as the handout's planning guidance, with the handout's own figures (about 80 to 90 kbps per G.711 call, about 25 to 30 kbps per G.729 call, a margin of at least 25 percent).
- The hard calculation item uses review question 10 on page 19 (20 compressed calls and 500 kbps with a 25 percent margin). The handout gives no answer, so the item assumes 30 kbps per call, the upper compressed figure that the page 13 worked example also uses, and says so in the prompt.
- The case scenario has no model answer. Hard items test only rules the handout states, applied to the case facts (injects 1 to 5, the asset list, the task list and the evaluation table). They do not ask for a group's plan.
- The Annex A estimate format and Annex B plan heading are tested only at the level of their main parts; Annex F abbreviations are used only to confirm terms such as PACE.
