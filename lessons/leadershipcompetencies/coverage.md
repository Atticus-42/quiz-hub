# POI 1.2 Military Leadership Competencies

Primary course source in the Leadership Phase folder: `2. Military Leadership Competencies (Leading, Developing, and Achieving).pptx` (47 slides). Citations use its original 1-based slide order.

Slides 12–16, 18, 21, 26, 29, 31–32 and 34 cover the competency model, its three categories, and their application at company level. The banks use the three-category model taught in this deck.

The film exercise and group presentation assessment remain separate classroom activities.

Each difficulty has 20 questions. Easy checks recall and recognition. Medium applies a principle in a classroom situation. Hard asks students to diagnose competing explanations, weigh tradeoffs, or integrate principles. The scenarios were written for this practice.
