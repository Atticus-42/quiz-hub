# NCO Empowerment coverage

POI 1.6 · Leadership

Primary source is `LEADERSHIP PHASE/6. NCO Empowerment.pptx` (50 slides).

Slide 6 asks students to develop NCO empowerment in an operational environment in accordance with FC 8-039. Slides 5 and 43 specify taking charge, NCO roles, duties, responsibilities, authority, inspections and corrections, and the company-level support channel.

Citations use the deck’s slide sequence. The bank uses fictional training cases and distinguishes correction, informal inspection, and readiness checks by their stated purposes. Slide 33 places a correction under an inspection heading, so the bank uses the definitions on slides 30–31 and avoids asking students to reproduce that classification. The written experience-based assessment on slide 44 is outside the multiple-choice format.
