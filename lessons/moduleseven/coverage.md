# Module 7 examination coverage

Approved hybrid design: 32 questions per difficulty, 8 primarily assessing each of all four lessons. Half of each share retains selected source-lesson foundations; half consists of new integrated scenarios. Primary lesson determines the score breakdown; each new scenario has support from at least two lessons. Existing lesson questions and histories remain unchanged.

Source files, numbering qualifications and substantive limitations are recorded in the corresponding lesson coverage reports: [NIST Framework](../nist/coverage.md), [Zero Trust](../zerotrust/coverage.md), [LOE3 GSOMIA Compliance](../gsomia/coverage.md), [Cyber Risk Assessment](../cyberrisk/coverage.md). Each source question below identifies the factual support and actual source pages/slides cited in the explanation. Scenario support is conceptual evidence, not a claim that the original slides contain the hypothetical vignette verbatim.

Difficulty progresses from concepts and direct application to diagnosis and evaluation under constraints. Classroom/organizational scenarios do not establish practical qualification, equipment readiness or legal release authority. Review the full lessons.
