# NIST Framework: coverage and source review

POI 7.1. Primary source: **1. NIST Framework.pdf** (31 pages). References use the file's actual page/slide position, not printed slide footers. Source files are not published.

## Scope and qualifications

The supplied lesson explicitly teaches CSF 2.0 and six Functions (pages 10 and 26). Its five-function introductory shorthand on page 2 is not used as a competing count. Tiers characterize rigor and integration, not a universal maturity score; clarification checked against NIST's official Framework Components FAQ. The seven-step sequence is the lesson's implementation approach, not a mandatory certification procedure. NICTEF statements are assessed as the supplied lesson's account, not current legal obligations. Historical background on page 16 is context, not date trivia. Study the full lesson; these single-best-answer exercises do not replace the instructor's essay examination. External clarification: [NIST Framework Components FAQ](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework/cybersecurity-framework-faqs-framework-components) and [CSF 2.0](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf).

## Assessment design

Easy checks comprehension, Medium applies concepts to a stated case, and Hard compares explanations or decisions under constraints. Each mode samples every declared topic. Four-option questions use one best answer; answer positions are balanced. Review full sources as well as the quiz.
