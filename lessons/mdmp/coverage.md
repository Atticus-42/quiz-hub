# POI 3.2 coverage

**Primary reference:** SOAC Subject 3.2 / SOBC Subject 2.2 *Military Decision-Making Process (MDMP)* student handout, 15-page PDF (`library/files/lesson-military-decision-making-process.pdf`), which follows PAM 3-0201 Chapter IX. Numbered references in the bank are PDF pages. Each difficulty has 25 questions: about half objective (steps, inputs and outputs, products, definitions, acronyms) and about half situational (Philippine Army planning-staff scenarios that ask which step, product or term applies).

**Answer balance:** every bank keeps its four answer letters within one question of each other. The correct answer is the unique longest or shortest option in no more than 35% of items. Prompts are unique, and every distractor comes from the same handout.

**Limits.**
- The handout refers to a Part V (practical exercise scenario) and a Part VI (worksheets), but the supplied PDF stops at Part IV and the self-check questions. The bank therefore tests only the steps, roles, outputs and Signal officer contributions printed on pages 1–15. It does not test any exercise parameters or worksheets.
- Only the S6 column of the step-by-step role matrix on page 13 is tested for per-step roles; the other columns are covered through the staff table on pages 5–6.
- Page 4 lists Step 4 outputs as refined COAs, potential decision points, war-game results, initial assessment measures and updated assumptions; page 10 names the synchronization matrix and the decision support template and matrix as war-game results. The bank tests the page 4 list and does not ask which of the two is "the" output.
- The self-check on page 15 asks for the "two key outputs of Step 1" although the table on page 3 lists one output (WARNORD 1). That item is not tested.
- The sample mission statement on page 9 and the doctrinal paragraph numbers are not tested. The 17 mission analysis actions are tested selectively rather than one by one. All scenarios are generic, and the "Exercise" unit names are fictional.
