# C4ISTAR Weapon System Integration: coverage and source review

POI 5.5. Primary source: **5. C4ISTAR Weapon System Integration.pptx** (40 slides). References use the file's actual page/slide position, not printed slide footers. Source files are not published.

## Scope and qualifications

Paired source: 5. Handout C4ISTAR Weapon System Integration.docx. References use actual slide positions in the 40-slide presentation. Assessment stays at conceptual system roles, information quality, interoperability, security, authority and historical capability evolution. No procedural targeting, firing, attack optimization, operational routing, real deployments or current system vulnerabilities are published. The four ELOs are covered at that conceptual level; practical configuration and weapon employment are not assessed by this public quiz. Dates and procurement totals that differ or are ambiguous across deck and handout are excluded. Historical examples are not claims about current operational readiness. Open architecture and TCP/IP do not by themselves ensure semantic interoperability or authorization. D3A/OODA appear only as named conceptual frameworks, not instructions for applying force.

## Assessment design

Easy checks comprehension, Medium applies concepts to a stated case, and Hard compares explanations or decisions under constraints. Each mode samples every declared topic. Four-option questions use one best answer; answer positions are balanced. Review full sources as well as the quiz.
