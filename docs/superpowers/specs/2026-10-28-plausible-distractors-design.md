# Plausible exam choices - approved design

The owner requests choices that do not reveal the correct answer through obvious distractors. The owner approved all 21 published lesson and module exams on 28 October 2026. Keep a single defensible best answer rather than introducing several correct choices.

## Evidence and intent

Read-only screening covered all 1,596 authored question records. Subject reviewers manually sampled 308 records across every bank and difficulty; this was not exhaustive manual validation. Recurring defects include unrelated alternatives (wallpaper, capitalization, purchase-price trivia), categorical wrong choices contrasted with a cautious key, unequal numbers of claims, and occasional overlapping answers. Most correct-answer-length flags alone do not establish a defect.

## Approaches

- Cosmetic word replacement or option reshuffling is inexpensive but does not repair implausible reasoning.
- Rewriting every question and stem would replace useful material and increase factual drift.
- Recommended: retain sound items and revise weak distractors across every bank/mode. Use credible mistakes from the same lesson, parallel scope and specificity, and minimally sharpen an ambiguous stem when necessary.

## Requirements

- Preserve counts, coverage, source-backed keyed concepts, difficulty identities, qids, histories and routes.
- Keep Easy recognizable with credible neighboring concepts; Medium applies concepts; Hard compares plausible decisions under stated conditions.
- Do not mechanically remove all absolute words or force every option to exactly the same length. Remove inappropriate cues, not meaningful distinctions.
- Every revised item must retain exactly one best answer. Do not make a legitimate alternative falsely wrong simply to retain an old key.
- Preserve source-reference separation and literal-text safety. No timer, schedule, history, visual redesign or Telegram changes.
- Rebuild automatic combined pools from their source banks; synchronize stored Module 5/7 foundation copies through their coverage provenance.
- Use automated cue screens as review aids, not proof of semantic quality. Independently review the actual changed items before publishing.

## Completion

Report what was actually reviewed and changed. Require preserved source support, unique keyed answers, balanced key positions, foundation provenance, a passing standard gate and browser smoke. The owner's approval includes publication after verification.
