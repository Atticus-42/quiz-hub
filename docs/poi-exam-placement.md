# POI placement and exam publishing rules

Owner-confirmed requirements, 25 October 2026. Consult this file when adding any lesson or revising existing exams.

Source: `Program of Instruction.pdf`, supplied by the owner and reviewed on 25 October 2026. This is a placement reference, not evidence that every listed lesson has source material or a published exam.

## Publishing policy

- Unsupported lessons stay absent from the student-facing site: no empty cards, disabled exams, "coming soon" or "materials needed" entries.
- Keep their intended placement here for future uploads. Match a new upload by its actual contents, not merely its folder number or filename.
- When sufficient teaching material arrives, map its topics to the corresponding POI entry below before authoring and publishing an exam.
- Do not invent missing lesson content, source citations, or questions simply to fill the POI.
- Each difficulty must contain 20–35 questions. Apply the owner's latest limit to new and revised existing exams, including the question count served by combined exams. Preserve topic coverage rather than truncating the first N questions.
- Use objective/comprehension questions and analytical/situational questions as appropriate. Each question needs a defensible answer, explanation and source reference.
- Maintain a coverage map against supplied lesson objectives and substantive topics. An outline alone does not support detailed procedural questions.
- Preserve stable URLs, lesson/history identifiers and historical results during display-name/module corrections. Do not reuse old question identifiers for unrelated questions.
- CipherDesk remains a standalone tool, not a module exam.
- These rules do not authorize Telegram announcements.
