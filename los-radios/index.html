01 / GET STARTED

From download to your first link.

  1. Install the APK

    On an Android 11+ phone, download the APK and open it from Downloads. If prompted, allow installation from that browser or file manager, then return and tap Install.

  2. Download a map

    Connect to the internet. Open the app, choose a map, select Test — Central Luzon, then Download. Allow about 86 MB for the terrain pack.

  3. Check a link

    In CHECK LINK, place ALPHA and BRAVO, choose the radios, then inspect the verdict and terrain profile. Missing terrain may show NO DATA; loading ground data may show PROVISIONAL.

  4. Explore and save

    Use COVERAGE → ANALYZE AREA for a site, radius or rectangle. Keep sites and links under SAVED for later review.

02 / PICK YOUR PLATFORM

Android or browser?

Android · take it offline

After the terrain pack is installed, calculations work offline within its coverage. Update the existing official app in place; avoid uninstalling if you want to keep saved data.

Browser · try it immediately

No APK installation. You need a connection to open or reload the browser app. Clearing browser storage can remove its saved data.

03 / KNOW THE COVERAGE

A preview, with clear limits.

The available test terrain covers Central Luzon, approximately 120–122° E and 14–16° N. It is not a nationwide terrain pack.

Use modeled results as planning estimates and confirm them with field measurements. This preview has not been confirmed through device field validation; Google Earth and ATAK/WinTAK export checks remain outstanding.
