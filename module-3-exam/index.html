Actual Exam Questions from the class’s graded exams

A separate category from Easy, Medium and Hard: 91 questions from 4 graded exams: Module Exam on Signal Support Integration (7 October 2026); Exam on Signal Support in Coalition Operations (7 October 2026); Exam on Signal Support in Joint Operations (5 October 2026); Exam on Signal Support in Combined Arms Operations (3 October 2026). Saved to the class history as Actual Exam attempts.

03

Records and privacy

No login and no tracking. Your answers stay in this browser tab and are cleared when you refresh or close it. When your instructor has set up the class history, finishing an attempt sends your name, difficulty, score, percentage, mastery band and finish time to the class history sheet, together with the reference codes of the questions you were asked and of those you missed (for the instructor's question analysis). The answers you chose are never sent, and practice retries of missed questions are not sent at all. To put questions you have not seen yet first, then those you missed, this browser remembers which questions it has shown you and which were missed (question codes only, no name); that record never leaves this device. Actual Exam attempts are sent to the class history the same way, labelled Actual Exam.
