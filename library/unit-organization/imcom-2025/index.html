Counting notes
  • Counts are the Required strength column of the TOE s-2025. Positions printed in parentheses are dual designations and are not counted. Officers (O1–O9) and enlisted personnel (EP, E1–E9) are read from the grade rows; civilian employees (SG grades) are shown separately and are not part of personnel totals (the printed aggregate total adds all three).
  • The elements sum to the UMD-counted strength of Installation Management Command (338 / 3,041 / 117; aggregate 3,496): officers 59 + 9 × 22 + 22 + 25 + 14 + 20 = 338, EP 269 + 9 × 188 + 363 + 449 + 225 + 43 = 3,041, civilians 48 + 9 × 4 + 0 + 0 + 14 + 19 = 117.
  • Mission: To manage, develop and administer military installations and reservations in order to perform Base Support and Systems Development (BSSD) function as directed by the GHQ, AFP. Basis of allocation: One (1) for the Philippine Army. Mobility 30%.
  • Reconciliation: the recap of the Installation Management Command (page 4) prints 338 / 3,041 / 117 (aggregate 3,496). The six child files add to the same figures when the Installation Management Battalion (nine, by its basis of allocation) is counted at 4 civilians, which is its printed civilian sub-total, its aggregate total and its manning documents. Counted at the 5 civilians given by its grade rows (see the discrepancy noted in that group) the children would add to 126 civilians.
  • Children link to the groups of the separate files; quantities are taken from the basis of allocation of each child.
