How to read this atlas

Counting notes
  • Counts are the Required strength column of the TOE s-2025. Positions printed in parentheses are dual designations and are not counted. Officers (O1–O9) and enlisted personnel (EP, E1–E9) are read from the grade rows; civilian employees (SG grades) are shown separately and are not part of personnel totals.
  • Unit boxes sum to the combat units: officers 3 × 15 + 9 × 30 + 3 × 5 = 330, EP 3 × 81 + 9 × 543 + 3 × 95 = 5,415, total 3 × 96 + 9 × 573 + 3 × 100 = 5,745. Both match the division recap (330 / 5,415 / 5,745), and the grade rows of the unit manning documents multiplied by 3, 9 and 3 equal every grade row of the division recap (for example O7 3, O6 6, O5 18, O4 27, O3 111, O2 51, O1 114).
  • The 2025 annex covers only the combat units (BDE HHQ x3, Infantry Battalion x9, Division Reconnaissance Company x3). The Division Headquarters, military intelligence, CMO, support, training and hospital units drawn in the 2019 division are not in these documents, so the figures are not comparable with the 2019 division total of 516 / 6,528.
  • The 2025 chart prints no strength figures in its boxes, only the quantities x3, x9 and x3; the totals come from the recap and the unit manning documents. The annex also prints no TOE number.
  • Against the same three unit types in the 2019 entry (3 × 15 + 9 × 32 + 3 × 5 = 348 officers, 3 × 83 + 9 × 570 + 3 × 95 = 5,664 EP): officers are 18 fewer and EP 249 fewer. The brigade headquarters company loses 2 EP each, the infantry battalion loses its Weapons Company strength (2 officers, 56 EP) and gains 29 EP in its headquarters company, and the reconnaissance company is unchanged at 5 / 95.

Counting notes
  • Counts are the Required strength column of the TOE s-2025. Positions printed in parentheses are dual designations and are not counted. Officers (O1–O9) and enlisted personnel (EP, E1–E9) are read from the grade rows; civilian employees (SG grades) are shown separately and are not part of personnel totals.
  • Elements sum to the battalion: officers 15 + 0 + 3 × 5 = 30, EP 153 + 0 + 3 × 130 = 543, total 168 + 3 × 135 = 573. These equal the battalion recap (30 / 543 / 573), the HHC recap (15 / 153 / 168) and the Rifle Company recap (5 / 130 / 135), and the grade rows of the HHC and rifle company UMDs (HHC + 3 × rifle) equal the battalion recap row by row.
  • The battalion Signal Platoon is 11 EP: platoon headquarters 1 (signal chief / platoon sergeant), communications section 6, electronics section 2, information systems section 2. The S6 section (signal officer, signal NCO, assistant signal NCO) is a separate 3-person staff section in the battalion headquarters, 1 officer and 2 EP. The platoon leader, supply sergeant and the electronics and information systems section chiefs are dual designations and are not counted.
  • Headquarters 10 / 30 plus headquarters company 5 / 123 give the HHC 15 / 153. The headquarters company holds company headquarters 7, support platoon 23 (including a 2-EP liaison section), medical platoon 17, signal platoon 11, intelligence platoon 23, scout platoon 26 and mortar platoon 21. The Sniper Section is entirely dual-designated (7 positions in parentheses) and adds 0. The UMD prints the collection teams (x3), scout teams (x3), rifle platoons (x3) and rifle squads (x9) as totals for all repeated elements; the tree shows the count for one element.
  • The Weapons Company is drawn as a dashed box on the battalion chart but has no basis of allocation, recap or manning document in the 2025 files, and the battalion recap equals the HHC plus three rifle companies. It is shown with no strength; its mortar platoon and sniper section now sit in the HHC.
  • Basis of allocation: nine Infantry Battalions per Infantry Division; one HHC per battalion (mobility 80%); three Rifle Companies per battalion (relies on the battalion headquarters). The battalion mobility is 30%. Mission: close with the enemy by fire and maneuver to destroy or capture him or repel his assault by fire, close combat and counterattack.
  • Compared with the 2019 entry (32 / 570 = 602): the officers drop by 2 and the EP by 27. The 2019 Weapons Company (2 / 56) is gone, the mortar platoon (21 EP) moves into the HHC, the sniper section changes from 7 counted EP to dual-designated, the S2 section gains 2 EP (5 to 7) and the intelligence platoon gains 6 EP (17 to 23: three collection teams of 7 against 4 plus a separate signal intelligence team of 3). Other HHC elements and the 5 / 130 Rifle Company are unchanged. The 2019 Heavy machine-gun platoon (18 EP) has no 2025 counterpart.
