How to read this atlas

These are source-edition teaching diagrams, not current unit rosters. Solid branches show the documented composition. Dashed branches identify attachments. A person shown inside a child element is already part of that element’s count.

Who does what? 7 key designations

Company commander

Responsible for employment, training, administration, personnel management and sustainment.

Executive officer

Second in command. Assists planning and coordinates sustainment.

Platoon leader

Responsible for the platoon’s employment, training, administration and logistics.

First sergeant

Senior company NCO. Supervises routine operations and advises the commander.

Platoon sergeant

Advises the platoon leader and supervises administration, logistics and maintenance.

Communications sergeant / senior radio operator

Supervises operation, maintenance and installation of organic communications.

Radio operator

Operates and maintains the assigned radio.
