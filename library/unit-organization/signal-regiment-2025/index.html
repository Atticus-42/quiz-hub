Counting notes
  • Counts are the Required strength column of the TOE s-2025. Positions printed in parentheses are dual designations and are not counted. Officers (O1–O9) and enlisted personnel (EP, E1–E9) are read from the grade rows; civilian employees (SG grades) are shown separately and are not part of personnel totals (the printed aggregate total adds all three).
  • The elements sum to the regiment recap (205 / 1,842 / 69; aggregate 2,116): officers 43 + 10 + 9 + 10 + 11 × 9 + 9 + 25 = 205, EP 97 + 21 + 110 + 117 + 11 × 115 + 112 + 120 = 1,842, civilians 6 + 4 + 14 + 13 + 11 + 7 + 14 = 69. The 2025 regiment chart prints no strength figures, so the check is against the recap by grade. The Signal Battalion is drawn with (x11); its basis of allocation reads eleven Signal Battalions. Battalion totals are taken from page 4 (recap by grade) of each battalion file; each battalion has its own plate.
  • Mission: to provide signal support for the command, control and administration of the Philippine Army. Basis of allocation: one Signal Regiment, PA. Mobility 50%. Capabilities include technological standards and doctrine on signal operations, signal units for HPA, Infantry Divisions and other major PA units, multi-level repair and maintenance of signal equipment, and fighting as infantry when required.
  • The HHC totals 43 / 97 / 6 (aggregate 146): Regiment Headquarters 40 / 68 / 5 (113) plus Headquarters Company 3 / 29 / 1 (33), each equal to its recap and to the grade rows of its UMD. The 17 office subtotals printed in the Regiment Headquarters UMD add to 113; the Office of the Chief of Staff has no subtotal of its own (its 2 positions are inside the 4 printed for the Secretary of the General Staff office), so the 1 / 1 split of those two offices is read from the billet rows. HHC mission: command, control and administration of the Regiment; basis of allocation one HHC; mobility 50%.
  • Dual designations (not counted): in the Regiment Headquarters, 6 officers, 2 EP and 3 civilians are printed in parentheses (Chief OESPA, the three SCDO section chiefs, GAD section chief, pre-audit section chief; assistant public accountability NCO and PAP monitoring NCO; Administrative Officer II and two Engineer II posts). No element has all its positions in parentheses, so no element carries dual figures. The Signal School has two O3 positions in parentheses (assistant heads of the Academic and Non-Academic Departments).
  • The Signal School totals 10 / 21 / 4 (aggregate 35): Headquarters 6 / 12 / 3 (Command Section 3 / 4, Administrative and Logistics Section 2 / 4, Operations and Training Section 1 / 4 / 3 civilians), Academic Department 1 / 3, Non-Academic Department 1 / 3, Doctrine and Research Department 2 / 3 / 1. Mission: specialized C4S education and training for PA personnel; one school for the Regiment; mobility 50%.
  • Compared with the 2019 entry (169 / 1,713 / 55): the 2025 regiment is 205 / 1,842 / 69. The new Cyber Battalion (25 / 120 / 14) is the largest change; the 2019 TO had no Cyber Battalion and kept cyber security as a company of the Network Enterprise Technology Battalion, which no longer appears in the 2025 NET Battalion. HHC rises from 34 / 87 / 6 to 43 / 97 / 6 (Regiment Headquarters 31 / 59 / 5 to 40 / 68 / 5; Headquarters Company 3 / 28 / 1 to 3 / 29 / 1). The Signal School rises from 9 / 21 / 4 to 10 / 21 / 4 and its 2019 Mobile Training Team box is not on the 2025 chart. The NET Battalion goes from 9 / 118 / 13 to 10 / 117 / 13. The Command Signal Battalion (9 / 110 / 14), Signal Battalion x11 (9 / 115 / 1) and Signal Installation and Maintenance Battalion (9 / 112 / 7) keep their 2019 totals. Net change: +36 officers, +129 EP, +14 civilians.
